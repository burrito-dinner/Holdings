The one-line thesis

Take a sized, survivable bet on a frontier network where the upside dwarfs the position.

Why I hold it

DNET is the asymmetric leg of the book. Where Bitcoin is the reserve, DNET is the wager — a frontier crypto position where I'm paid for understanding something the market hasn't fully priced. The job of this position is to be wrong cheaply and right enormously.

  • Convexity: small enough to lose, large enough to matter if it works.
  • Edge: a thesis grounded in the network's mechanics, not the narrative.
  • Optionality: exposure to an outcome BTC alone can't provide.

Position sizing

Deliberately sub-reserve. DNET is sized so that total loss is survivable and never threatens the core, while a success re-rates the entire book. It must continually out-earn Bitcoin on a risk-adjusted basis to keep its weight.

What I'm watching

  • Real usage and retention, not speculative volume.
  • Token-holder alignment and credible decentralisation over time.
  • Whether the network's moat widens or narrows each cycle.

What would change my mind

  • The core thesis breaking — adoption stalls or the moat collapses.
  • Governance or supply dynamics turning against holders.
  • A cleaner expression of the same bet appearing elsewhere.

Risk framing

This position can go to zero, and the book is built to absorb that. The only unacceptable outcome is being forced out of a correct thesis by mis-sizing — so it is sized to be held through maximum pain.
