Why I hold it

Bitcoin is the anchor of this balance sheet because it is the only asset I can hold with no counterparty, no issuer, and a supply schedule no one can change. In a world of expanding balance sheets, a fixed twenty-one-million-coin cap is a bet on arithmetic over politics.

  • Scarcity — a hard cap enforced by code and consensus, not promises.
  • Neutrality — no chief executive, no foundation, no jurisdiction to capture.
  • Liquidity — deep, global, around the clock; exit is always available.
  • Sovereignty — self-custodied keys; the position is mine, fully.

Position sizing

BTC is sized as the reserve — the largest single weight in the book — precisely because it carries the lowest idiosyncratic risk of anything I own. It is the benchmark; DNET must out-earn it to justify its convexity.

What would change my mind

  • A credible break in the issuance schedule or twenty-one-million cap.
  • Sustained, structural loss of hashrate decentralisation.
  • A superior, more credibly neutral settlement layer at scale.

Risk framing

Drawdowns of seventy percent or more are part of the contract, not a surprise. The position is sized so that a brutal cycle never forces a sale. Time in the asset, not timing of the asset, is the edge.
