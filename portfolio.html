BitcoinBTC

Role — Reserve Class — Crypto · L1 Custody — Cold / self Conviction — Highest

The foundation of the book. Bitcoin is held as a long-duration reserve asset — the hardest, most liquid, most credibly neutral money yet engineered — and the benchmark against which every other position must justify its risk.

Portfolio allocation 65% of book
Own the scarcest monetary asset in existence, and refuse to be shaken out of it.

Why I hold it

Bitcoin is the anchor of this balance sheet because it is the only asset I can hold with no counterparty, no issuer, and a supply schedule no one can change. In a world of expanding balance sheets, a fixed twenty-one-million-coin cap is a bet on arithmetic over politics.

  • Scarcity — a hard cap enforced by code and consensus, not promises.
  • Neutrality — no chief executive, no foundation, no jurisdiction to capture.
  • Liquidity — deep, global, around the clock; exit is always available.
  • Sovereignty — self-custodied keys; the position is mine, fully.

Position sizing

BTC is sized as the reserve — the largest single weight in the book — precisely because it carries the lowest idiosyncratic risk of anything I own. It is the benchmark; DNET must out-earn it to justify its convexity.

What would change my mind

  • A credible break in the issuance schedule or twenty-one-million cap.
  • Sustained, structural loss of hashrate decentralisation.
  • A superior, more credibly neutral settlement layer at scale.

Risk framing

Drawdowns of seventy percent or more are part of the contract, not a surprise. The position is sized so that a brutal cycle never forces a sale. Time in the asset, not timing of the asset, is the edge.

DNETDNET

Role — Convexity Class — Frontier crypto Variance — High Conviction — High

The convex position. DNET is the book's asymmetric bet — sized smaller than the reserve, but carrying the upside a concentrated, sovereign balance sheet exists to capture. A living thesis that updates as the network proves itself.

Portfolio allocation 35% of book
Take a sized, survivable bet on a frontier network where the upside dwarfs the position.

Why I hold it

DNET is the asymmetric leg of the book. Where Bitcoin is the reserve, DNET is the wager — a frontier crypto position where I am paid for understanding something the market has not fully priced. The job of this position is to be wrong cheaply and right enormously.

  • Convexity — small enough to lose, large enough to matter if it works.
  • Edge — a thesis grounded in the network's mechanics, not the narrative.
  • Optionality — exposure to an outcome Bitcoin alone cannot provide.

Position sizing

Deliberately sub-reserve. DNET is sized so that total loss is survivable and never threatens the core, while a success re-rates the entire book. It must continually out-earn Bitcoin on a risk-adjusted basis to keep its weight.

What I'm watching

  • Real usage and retention, not speculative volume.
  • Token-holder alignment and credible decentralisation over time.
  • Whether the network's moat widens or narrows each cycle.

Risk framing

This position can go to zero, and the book is built to absorb that. The only unacceptable outcome is being forced out of a correct thesis by mis-sizing — so it is sized to be held through maximum pain.
