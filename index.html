Article II — The Doctrine Four principles, no committee

Rules the house
never breaks.

Principle I.

Sovereignty

Self-custodied, self-funded, self-directed. No outside capital means no outside pressure — decisions answer to conviction alone.

Principle II.

Concentration

Diversification is a hedge against ignorance. A small number of deeply understood positions, sized with intent.

Principle III.

Patience

Measured in cycles, not quarters. Volatility is the toll paid for asymmetric returns, not a reason to flinch.

Principle IV.

Asymmetry

Risk what is knowable; chase what is convex. Capped downside, uncapped upside — every position must earn its place.

Article III — The Mandate

No outside capital. No clients. Only conviction, given time enough to compound.

BFD Holdings exists to compound one person's conviction over a very long horizon. It does not solicit, accept, or manage outside capital. No clients to serve, no redemptions to fear — only positions to understand, and time to let them work.
