Investment Philosophy

How We Think About
Capital Allocation

Our investment philosophy is grounded in three core principles that have guided every decision at BFD Holdings since inception.

01
🔍

Fundamental Research

We build investment theses from the ground up — understanding businesses, industries, and competitive dynamics before a single dollar is committed. Our 28-person research team conducts over 1,200 company meetings annually.

02
⚙️

Disciplined Process

Repeatable, rigorous investment frameworks eliminate emotional decision-making. Every position is stress-tested under multiple scenarios, and our risk committee reviews all new mandates before capital deployment.

03
🕰️

Long-Term Perspective

We are patient capital. Our average holding period exceeds five years, allowing us to capture the full compounding power of great businesses while avoiding the friction costs of excessive turnover.
