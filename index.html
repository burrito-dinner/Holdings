What we do

We hold few things, and hold them absolutely.

A deliberately small book of liquid digital assets — Bitcoin as the reserve, DNET as the frontier wager — self-custodied, unlevered, and measured in cycles rather than quarters. Nothing is managed for anyone else.

Operating principles
01 Sovereignty Self-custodied, self-funded, self-directed. No outside capital means no outside pressure.
02 Concentration Diversification hedges ignorance. A small number of deeply understood positions, sized with intent.
03 Patience Measured in cycles, not quarters. Volatility is the toll paid for asymmetric returns.
04 Asymmetry Capped downside, uncapped upside — every position must earn its place on the sheet.
