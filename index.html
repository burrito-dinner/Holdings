Operating doctrine

How this balance
sheet is run.

Four principles. No committee, no consensus to dilute them.

01

Sovereignty

Self-custodied, self-funded, self-directed. No outside capital means no outside pressure — decisions answer to conviction alone.

02

Concentration

Diversification is a hedge against ignorance. A small number of deeply understood positions, sized with intent.

03

Long horizon

Measured in cycles, not quarters. Volatility is the toll paid for asymmetric returns, not a reason to flinch.
